# Year-2
Projects from academic year 2 (2024-2025).

## What the year entailed:
In the second year, the programme shifted from foundational skills toward applied, client-facing projects with real research and industry partners. Blocks combined mixed-methods research (quantitative and qualitative), advanced computer vision and robotics, natural language processing with transformer models, and full MLOps deployment pipelines. Students worked directly with external organisations—including Digiwerkplaats MKB and the Netherlands Plant Eco-phenotyping Centre (NPEC)—to deliver production-oriented solutions, moving from proof-of-concept notebooks to cloud-deployed, monitored, and continuously retrained systems. Agile/Scrum project management, technical documentation, and stakeholder communication were embedded throughout.

## Highlights
- Conducted mixed-methods research (86-respondent survey + 10 interviews) translating consumer trust findings into ML-based cybersecurity recommendations for SMEs
- Built a custom U-Net segmentation model (F1 = 0.8374) and A*-based root architecture extraction pipeline for high-throughput plant phenotyping
- Developed and benchmarked PID vs. Reinforcement Learning controllers for robotic plant inoculation, achieving sub-millimeter positioning accuracy (0.592mm)
- Designed a full NLP pipeline—from ASR and translation to transformer-based emotion classification—processing multilingual video content
- Took ownership of the Azure ML training pipeline for a production computer vision system, implementing automated hyperparameter tuning, scheduled retraining, and experiment tracking
- Delivered projects end-to-end with client organisations (Digiwerkplaats MKB, NPEC), from research proposals to cloud-deployed, monitored applications
